# 30-Day SAA-C03 Practice Roadmap

**Start:** 2026-10-16 · **End:** 2026-11-14 · **Reflection:** 2026-11-15

## Domains

- D1: Design Secure Architectures — 30%.
- D2: Design Resilient Architectures — 26%.
- D3: Design High-Performing Architectures — 24%.
- D4: Design Cost-Optimized Architectures — 20%.

Each week touches all four domains. The aim is **16 evidence-based activities**, not 16 live deployments.

## Setup: Oct 16–18

- Inspect account free-plan status, credit, expiration and MFA; do not publish billing identity.
- Choose a Region; check AWS Budgets support and relevant pricing.
- Prepare Git, Python, AWS CLI and credential hygiene.
- Solve 20 baseline practice questions; track error categories, not copyrighted question text.

## Week 1: Foundation — Oct 19–24

- D1-L1: IAM least privilege with denied access proof.
- D2-L1: S3 versioning and object recovery.
- D3-L1: S3 request-pattern measurements.
- D4-L1: budgets and storage/request cost drivers.

## Week 2: Builder — Oct 26–31

- D1-L2: least-privilege Lambda role.
- D2-L2: idempotent event handling.
- D3-L2: Lambda + DynamoDB timing.
- D4-L2: Lambda vs. EC2 cost model.

## Week 3: Architect — Nov 2–7

- D1-L3: threat model for the queue workload.
- D2-L3: SQS + DLQ failure handling.
- D3-L3: batch and concurrency study.
- D4-L3: synchronous vs. asynchronous costs.

## Week 4: Professional — Nov 9–14

- D1-L4: encryption and threat-model review.
- D2-L4: Multi-AZ and RTO/RPO recovery **design**.
- D3-L4: data and caching alternatives.
- D4-L4: three workload cost scenarios.

## Sundays and review

Oct 25, Nov 1, Nov 8 and Nov 15: lightweight weekly reviews. Assess conceptual accuracy, local/cloud evidence, diagrams, real spend, cleanup and portfolio quality.
